## 1. One line

**Cliprame is personal video for sales and outreach: record a walkthrough in one take, send one link, and know the moment your prospect watches it.**

Tagline used across the site: **"Send a video. Know who watched."**
Footer line: **"Record. Send. Know who watched."**

## 2. Elevator pitches

**10 seconds**
Cliprame lets founders and salespeople send a short personal video instead of another follow-up email, and pings them in Slack the moment the prospect presses play.

**30 seconds**
Follow-up emails get skimmed. A two-minute personal video gets watched. Cliprame is a browser studio for recording that video — with a teleprompter only you can see and editing by deleting words — and a tracked link for sending it. You see who watched, how far they got, which part they rewatched and whether they booked a call, and Slack or HubSpot hears about it in real time. Lock confidential proposals with a PIN or email code, or put a lead form in front of the video that posts straight into HubSpot with UTM tracking.

**60 seconds (problem → solution → why us)**
Sales and founder-led outreach runs on email, and email is invisible: you never know if the proposal was opened, skimmed or forwarded to the decision-maker. Personal video works, but today it means juggling a recorder, a teleprompter app, an editor, a file host and a spreadsheet of who-watched-what. Cliprame does all of it in one browser tab. Record your screen and face in one take with private talking points on screen; fix mistakes by deleting words from the transcript; send each prospect their own link with their name on the page. Then Cliprame tells you exactly what happened: a heatmap of where people rewatched, skipped or dropped off, every viewer by name, button clicks, form fills and Calendly bookings — pushed to Slack, HubSpot or any CRM via webhook. It's built in India, priced for Indian teams, supports Hindi and Hinglish captions, and respects viewer privacy (Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured).

## 3. The problem

- **Outreach is invisible.** "Did they open it? Did the decision-maker see it?" Sales teams follow up blind.
- **Email is skimmed, PDFs aren't read.** Proposals and pricing sit unopened or are read without context.
- **Recording good video is awkward.** People don't send video because they ramble, restart, and then have to edit.
- **The tools are scattered.** Recorder + teleprompter + editor + video host + analytics + CRM sync = five products and a spreadsheet.
- **Confidential material leaks.** Pricing and proposals get forwarded to people they weren't meant for.

## 5. How it works (three steps)

1. **Record in one take.** Screen and camera together, with a teleprompter only you can see. Fluffed a line? Delete it from the transcript and the video closes the gap.
2. **Send one link.** Each prospect gets their own page with their name on it. Paste it into email as a clickable thumbnail, or drop it into LinkedIn or Slack. Optionally lock it or put a form in front of it.
3. **Know who watched.** A Slack message the moment they press play. Then the details: how far they got, what they rewatched, whether they clicked your button, filled in your form or booked a call.

## 6. Feature catalogue

Each feature: **what it does** → *why it matters*.

### 6.1 Record (browser studio, no install)
- **Screen + camera in one take** with a floating picture-in-picture camera card → *your face stays next to the product, proposal or deck.*
- **Private teleprompter** that scrolls on screen while recording and never appears in the video → *sound prepared, not scripted.*
- **AI talking points / scripts** in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil and Telugu (Gemini) → *start from a draft instead of a blank page.*
- **Showcase frames**: wrap the screen in a browser window, macOS window or minimal card on styled backgrounds.
- **Background effects**: blur, green-screen-free cutout, custom image (runs on-device with MediaPipe).
- **Hand-tracked air drawing**: circle a number or underline a plan by drawing in the air; rough shapes snap clean.
- **Creative looks**: hologram, comic, sketch and anime camera styles.
- **Clean audio**: noise suppression and echo cancellation.

### 6.2 Edit
- **Edit by text**: the recording is transcribed word by word; select words and delete them to cut the video; anything removed can be restored → *fix mistakes like editing a doc.*
- **Word-timed captions** (Auto, English, Hindi, Hinglish) → *videos work on mute.*
- Timeline trim, split and cuts; speed; zoom; colour grading; style presets; transitions; text overlays; music.
- **Delivery Coach**: finds filler words ("um", "basically") and long pauses.
- **AI voiceover / dubbing**: translate and voice in English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu (male or female voice).

### 6.3 Send & share
- **Hosted videos** with private storage and short-lived playback links.
- **Personal links** per prospect: the page greets them by name ("Hi Ana, this video is for you"), and their views are attributed to them.
- **Email-ready thumbnail**: copy a clickable image with a play button that pastes into Gmail/Outlook.
- **Chapters** so busy viewers can jump to pricing, and you see which chapters each person chose.
- **Call-to-action button** under the video (e.g. "Book a call").
- **Embed on your website** with a script tag or iframe, inline or as a popup button; views on each website are tracked separately. Paid plans can restrict embeds to their own domains.
- Viewers **don't need an account**.

### 6.4 Viewer analytics
- **Every viewer, by name** (via personal links, email codes or gate forms; everyone else grouped by browser), with device, country and a timeline of what they did.
- **Audience retention curve** with chapters marked.
- **Drop-off heatmap**: three rows showing where viewers **re-watched**, **skipped ahead** or **dropped off**; click a moment to play it → *see exactly which part of the pitch landed or lost them.*
- **Chapter interest**: share of viewers who reached each chapter, watched it, rewatched it.
- **Where views came from**: share link vs each website the video is embedded on.
- **Button clicks, form submissions and bookings** on each viewer's timeline.
- Fake-view protection: link previews and bots are ignored, playback can't be inflated faster than real time, and new sessions are rate-limited per network.

### 6.5 Interactive video & lead capture
- **Forms inside the video**, placed **before** the video (a real gate — the video isn't sent until the form is submitted), **at a timestamp** (video pauses; skippable or required), or **at the end**.
- **User-defined fields**: text, email, phone, long text, dropdown, checkbox, each with its own field name (HubSpot property presets: `email`, `firstname`, `lastname`, `company`, `jobtitle`, `phone`).
- **Hidden fields** filled on the server at submission: `{utm_source}`, `{utm_medium}`, `{utm_campaign}`, `{utm_term}`, `{utm_content}`, any `{param:name}` from the link, `{referrer}`, `{page_url}`, `{video_title}`, `{link_name}`, `{link_email}`, `{link_company}`, `{source}`, `{date}` → *track the full journey; viewers can't see or tamper with them.* Embedded videos pass the **host page's** UTM tags.
- **Forward submissions** to a **HubSpot form** (portal ID + form ID, no token needed) or **any URL** (JSON or form-encoded: Salesforce Web-to-Lead, Pardot, Zapier, Make, Formspree).
- **Leads table** per video with answers, hidden values, forwarding status and **CSV export**.
- **Timed buttons** (e.g. "See plans" at the pricing moment) and **booking embeds** (Calendly, Cal.com, HubSpot Meetings) inside the player; **Calendly bookings are detected automatically**.
- Spam protection: honeypot field and per-network rate limits.

### 6.6 Protection for confidential pitches
- **PIN** (6–8 digits), **password**, or **email one-time code** before a video plays.
- Email codes can be limited to **named people or whole companies** (`@northwind.com`); personal links sent to an address only open for that address.
- The **video isn't in the page** until the viewer unlocks it; link previews show no frames.
- Wrong guesses are rate-limited; changing the lock signs everyone out; links can be switched off instantly.
- Viewers who unlock by email appear by name in analytics.

### 6.7 Real-time alerts & integrations
- **Slack**: a message in a channel when someone starts watching, watches to the end, clicks the button, fills in a form or books a call.
- **HubSpot**: a note on the contact's timeline for every moment (creates the contact if needed); form answers included.
- **Signed webhooks** (HMAC-SHA256, Stripe-style signature) for Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zapier, Make, n8n or custom code.
- Each moment is sent **once per viewing**; "named viewers only" option skips anonymous views; test button; delivery status shown; duplicates prevented.
- Guided setup with step-by-step instructions and a live preview of the Slack message, HubSpot note or JSON.
- Integration secrets are encrypted at rest; webhooks can't target private networks.

### 6.8 Photo + video reels (secondary use)
- Templates, motion and transitions, depth-based camera moves (dolly, orbit, sway) from flat photos, AI on-screen captions and AI voiceovers. Useful for product showcases and social teasers. *Not the lead message of the brand any more.*

### 6.9 Chrome extension — Cliprame Recorder
- Records any browser tab with camera bubble and voice (up to 1080p, 30 fps), keyboard shortcut ⌥⇧R, pause/resume, countdown that never appears in the video.
- **Privacy masks applied before capture**: pick elements to hide, or auto-mask emails, phone numbers, card numbers, API keys/tokens (Stripe, AWS, GitHub, Google, Slack, OpenAI, JWTs), PAN and Aadhaar numbers, custom words and form fields → *record your real dashboard without leaking customer data.*
- Recordings open in the Cliprame editor.

### 6.10 Account, onboarding & plans
- Sign-in with password or one-time email code.
- **Account page** with profile, plan and usage meters (videos hosted, transcription and voiceover minutes this month, storage used), renewal/cancellation, password, and account deletion.
- **Guided product tours** on first visit (Home, video page, Integrations), replayable from the account menu.
- Installable **PWA**.

### 6.11 Trust & safety (admin)
- **Report this video** link on every share page (no account needed; urgent reasons for non-consensual intimate content and child safety).
- Super-admin dashboard: users, plans, revenue, storage, AI usage and cost (in **USD or INR**), reports queue, **video takedowns**, **account bans** (reversible), and a full **audit log**. Admins only open a video to review a report, via a 15-minute link that is logged.

## 10. Privacy, security & compliance

- Written for **India's DPDP Act 2023** and **GDPR**; privacy policy, terms and a data processing agreement are published. For viewer data, the sender is the controller and Cliprame the processor.
- Videos in a **private bucket** (Cloudflare R2), played through expiring links. Database access restricted per account (row-level security).
- PINs, passwords and email codes stored only as hashes; integration tokens encrypted at rest (AES-GCM).
- Viewer tracking is disclosed on every share page; **GPC / Do Not Track** turn it off; network addresses only stored as salted hashes.
- Content reporting with urgent handling, reversible takedowns and bans, and an audit log of every admin action (IT Rules 2021 intermediary obligations).
- Sub-processors: Supabase, Cloudflare R2, Google (Gemini), Razorpay, Vercel, Resend (email codes), and optionally Sentry and PostHog.

## 16. Glossary

- **Personal link**: a share link made for one recipient; their views are attributed to them.
- **Gate form**: a form before the video; the video only loads after it's submitted.
- **Hidden field**: a value sent with a form submission that the viewer doesn't see (e.g. `utm_source`).
- **Moment**: an event that can trigger an alert — started watching, watched to the end, clicked the button, filled in a form, booked a call.
- **Heatmap**: per-slice share of viewers who re-watched, skipped or dropped off.
- **Named viewer**: a viewer identified by personal link, email code or form email.
- **Takedown**: an admin removing a video for breaking the content rules (reversible).
